# Demonstration scenarios

## 1_search_and_suggest

**Do:** Type 'Bella' in the search box. Suggestions appear with name, hierarchy and type. Choose 'Bellandur Lake'.

**Expect:** Globe flies to the lake; the outline shown is derived from JRC water occurrence (solid), never invented.

## 2_historical_analysis_bellandur

**Do:** Press Explore for 2002 to 2022. Open the Details tab.

**Expect:** Illustrative Dataset badge; water, lake extent, aquatic vegetation, wetland and built-up can be selected under Measure. Lake change is modest and separate from the surroundings.

## 3_official_hyderabad

**Do:** Search 'Hyderabad', 2000 to 2020.

**Expect:** Satellite Observations badge (GHSL built-up, epochs 2000, 2010, 2020). The timeline has three real dates.

## 4_himalayas

**Do:** Search 'Himalayas' or say 'Show the Himalayas'.

**Expect:** Illustrative Dataset: glacier, snow, glacial lake and exposed terrain are separate measures.

## 5_no_history

**Do:** Search 'Chennai'.

**Expect:** Place is shown; banner says Data Unavailable; no timeline, no analysis, no fictional history.

## 6_parent_area

**Do:** Search 'Uttarakhand'.

**Expect:** No dataset covers the whole state; chips offer the dataset that exists inside it.

## 7_voice

**Do:** Press the microphone and say 'Compare this location between 2000 and 2020'.

**Expect:** Same function as the Explore button runs; the confirmation text shows what was understood.

## 8_back_forward

**Do:** After two searches press Back, then Forward.

**Expect:** Location, years, measure, map mode, timeline date and camera are restored.

## 9_insufficient

**Do:** Open the Sundarbans WorldCover dataset for 2000 to 2024.

**Expect:** Single-date data: outcome is insufficient_data with a reason, never 'zero change'.
